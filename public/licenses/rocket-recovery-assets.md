# Rocket recovery imagery and model credits

Original Three.js geometry and procedural material textures by How Things Click. The Falcon 9 and Flight 5-era Super Heavy models use public exterior references, not SpaceX CAD.

## Starbase aerial imagery
- Local file: /textures/rocket-recovery/starbase-aerial.jpg (2048 × 2048).
- Source: USGS National Map Imagery service / NAIP, accessed 2026-09-11.
- Service: https://basemap.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSImageryOnly/MapServer
- Export: 4400 × 4400 Web Mercator metres around 25.9971° N, 97.1569° W.
- USGS describes this CONUS imagery as public domain: https://www.usgs.gov/national-map-supporting-themes
- This is historical geographic context. The foreground tower, roads, tanks and service buildings are an illustrative reconstruction, not a current site survey.

No third-party video footage, paid models or live data APIs are incorporated. SpaceX, Falcon 9 and Super Heavy identify the systems explained; this independent educational project is not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX, NASA or USGS.

The simulation is reduced-order final-approach guidance, not mission telemetry or proprietary flight-control software. Atlantic drone-ship position is illustrative and varies by mission.
