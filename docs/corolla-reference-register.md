## Visually inspected suspension catalog diagram

[2025 Corolla front suspension components](https://www.toyotapartsnow.com/parts-list/2025-toyota-corolla/suspension/front-suspension-components), first exploded diagram, was opened successfully as a public image and visually inspected on 2026-09-06. It shows the stamped lower arm, two different bushing mounts, separately bolted ball joint, and cast knuckle with a central hub bore, two strut fasteners and a steering-arm boss. The catalog contains several build/trim variants; its US-built 2.0-litre fitment entries were read separately. This drawing establishes component form and relationships, not measured dimensions or exact VIN fitment.

Applied: replace the earlier tubular arm and block knuckle, add separate forward/rear bushings, ball joints and hollow hub bearings on both sides. Spring paths now share the damper axis and include seats and protective bellows. These shapes are original reconstructions, not copied diagram textures.

## Exterior review, 6 September 2026

- Toyota's [2025 US Corolla brochure](https://www.toyota.com/content/dam/toyota/brochures/pdf/2025/corolla_ebrochure.pdf), printed page 21, specifies LE tread of 60.3 inches front / 61.0 rear and 10.8-inch ventilated front / 10.2-inch solid rear discs. These replace the single track value and oversized brake geometry; tests measure the actual generated geometry.
- The original [42602-02540 wheel-cover photograph](https://suburbanwheelcover.com/products/2020-2025-toyota-corolla-le-16-hubcap-42602-02540) was visually inspected. It has eight broad faceted spokes, eight rounded openings, a continuous outer shoulder and five recessed lug openings. This corrects the previous ten-spoke approximation. The photograph guides shape; the seller's fitment range is not independent verification of a 2025 VIN.
- Supplied side and quarter photographs guide the black rear quarter-window divider, softer lower door stamping and painted mirror caps. The mirror backing, glass and adjustment mechanism now separate. The hidden adjustment unit remains illustrative.
- Four removable wheel-house liners and their return flanges close the wheel openings. Upper door surrounds and B-pillar appliques now belong to the removable doors; the fixed pillar structure stays with the passenger cell.
- Studio reflections are generated in code, without downloaded textures. This improves paint and glass presentation but does not establish photographic or factory-CAD accuracy.

## Fidelity boundary and next work

### Cabin seating, 7 September 2026

- The user's supplied front-cabin photograph guides two-tone cloth, dark centre/shoulder regions, light side bolsters, rounded head restraints with exposed posts, the three-spoke wheel and centre console. Surface contours and dimensions remain reconstructed from the image.
- Toyota's [2025 Corolla owner manual, OM02688U](https://assets.sia.toyota.com/publications/en/om-s/OM02688U/pdf/OM02688U.pdf), printed pages 114–115, was rendered and visually inspected. The diagrams show separate backrest release buttons; the right-backrest centre-belt caution supports placing the centre belt with the wider right section. The implementation uses left 40% / right 60% ownership.
- Printed page 279 was also rendered and visually inspected: the rear centre armrest folds forward and contains two cup recesses. The model includes the stowed armrest and a separately removable cup insert; it does not yet animate the folding mechanism. Exact hinge and latch dimensions remain unverified.
- The [2025 US Corolla brochure](https://www.toyota.com/content/dam/toyota/brochures/pdf/2025/corolla_ebrochure.pdf) confirms LE fabric seating, a 60/40 rear seat, rear centre armrest/cup holders and steering-wheel controls. Steering switch packs are separate selectable assemblies; their small control legends remain illustrative.

### Front cover and impact absorber, 6 September 2026

- The supplied exact EchoPark front, quarter and side photographs guide the diagonal bumper/fender joint, long lower corner crease and rounded lower door boundaries. Shared profiles now define both the removable panels and visible shut lines.
- Toyota dealer [52611-02500 absorber listing](https://parts.elmhursttoyota.com/oem-parts/toyota-bumper-impact-absorber-5261102500) identifies the US-built application excluding SE/XSE/Nightshade/Apex/FX. The listing establishes configuration, not independent VIN certification.
- A [physical part photograph](https://www.ebay.com/itm/156484110132), [direct image](https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/PJsAAOSwr7tnIXQj/s-l1200.jpg), was visually inspected. Its stepped ends, two large end openings, central recesses and circular mould impressions guide the original foam geometry. No photograph is used as an in-product texture.
- Front bumper catalog text was accessible, but the requested exploded image was blocked/unavailable. Do not describe that diagram as visually verified. The hollow reinforcement, stays, fasteners and front-rail sweep are spatial reconstructions; exact section material, dimensions and crash response are unverified.

This is an original procedural educational model. Parts diagrams identify assemblies and attachment relationships; photographs guide surfaces. Neither supplies measured 3D contours, all brackets, every fastener or manufacturing tolerances. The exterior, seats, trim, HVAC cases, engine castings and harness routing still require iterative visual comparison. The project must not claim factory CAD accuracy or that every production component is modeled.

The generic V6 systems graphic is a completeness checklist only. The correct model uses a transverse inline-four and a front transaxle with two half shafts; it has no longitudinal V6/driveshaft layout.
